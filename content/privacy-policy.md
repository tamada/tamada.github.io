## :lock: Privacy Policy

This policy describes how this site (https://tamada.github.io/) handles information about visitors.

### Information we collect

This site does not ask visitors for personal information, and it has no registration or comment features.

### Access analysis

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by Google LLC, to understand how the site is used.
GA4 uses cookies and similar technologies to collect traffic data such as pages viewed, referrers, approximate location, browser, and device information.
The data is collected anonymously and does not identify individual visitors.

The collected data is used only to improve the site.
It is processed by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
See also [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can opt out of GA4 by disabling cookies in your browser settings or by installing the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Third-party services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may collect access logs such as IP addresses.
See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.
Links to external sites are governed by the privacy policies of those sites.

### Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised without prior notice.
The latest version is always published on this page.
